## G — Governance and continuity

### G-01: Leadership coverage and decision boundaries

**Purpose**  
Keep a large operating site stable when leadership roles or staffing change.

**Work completed / established**

- Leadership work has been separated into patient-value/KPI, daily operations, safety/management, inventory, training, and escalation domains.
- The need for more than one decision-maker during operating hours has been documented.
- Formal authority and practical operating judgment have been treated as different responsibilities.
- Backup coverage during absence has been recognized as an explicit design requirement.

**Current issue**

- Some work remains attached to individuals instead of roles.
- The boundary between the site lead, deputies, experienced staff, and formally accountable staff is not consistently explicit.
- A smaller formal leadership structure may appear efficient while increasing hidden escalation load and single-person dependence.

**Next actions**

- Map recurring decisions by risk: routine, conditional delegation, leadership approval, and formal-authority only.
- Assign a primary role and backup role to each high-frequency decision category.
- Separate handoffs by workstream rather than using one broad transition note.

**Completion condition**

- Every critical decision category has an owner, backup, escalation condition, and review route.

### G-02: Protected management time

**Purpose**  
Prevent management, analysis, staff support, and compliance work from being displaced by routine frontline work.

**Work completed / established**

- Protected management time has been identified as a recurring operational requirement rather than discretionary desk time.
- KPI ownership has been distributed by domain while the site lead retains portfolio-level accountability.

**Next actions**

- Fix recurring management blocks within working hours.
- Define what can interrupt the block and what must wait.
- Review whether the block produced a decision, completed document, feedback action, or risk reduction.

**Completion condition**

- Management time is scheduled, used, and reviewed as an operational capacity item.

### G-03: Transition and handoff control

**Purpose**  
Complete staffing or role transitions without mixing personnel communication, task transfer, and patient-facing arrangements.

**Work completed / established**

- Handoffs are being separated into operational duties, compliance duties, open cases, stakeholder communication, and access/asset return.
- Communication timing is treated as a controlled decision.

**Completion condition**

- Each departing responsibility has a named role-level successor, evidence of transfer, and a closure check.

## S — Safety and compliance

### S-01: High-risk work review

**Purpose**  
Reduce the risk that one person performs, approves, and reviews the same high-risk process.

**Work completed / established**

- High-risk tasks are being assessed using separation of approval, execution, and review.
- Formal-accountability tasks are distinguished from routine tasks that can be delegated.

**Next actions**

- List high-risk workflows.
- Define mandatory independent checks and escalation triggers.
- Keep audit evidence lightweight but retrievable.

**Completion condition**

- High-risk workflows have documented control points and no unreviewed single-person path.

### S-02: Near-miss learning and standard work

**Purpose**  
Turn incidents and near misses into operational changes, not only individual reminders.

**Work completed / established**

- Short daily briefings have been introduced.
- Structured review checklists and digital documentation support have been adopted.
- Countermeasures are considered across workflow, responsibility, environment, education, and custom.

**Next actions**

- Confirm whether each countermeasure changes the work at the point of risk.
- Check for recurrence and unintended burden.
- Retire controls that add work without reducing risk.

**Completion condition**

- Each material event produces an assigned system change, follow-up date, and effectiveness check.

### S-03: Boundaries for exceptional service requests

**Purpose**  
Avoid unsafe or unsustainable personalized arrangements that depend on one employee.

**Work completed / established**

- Individual accommodation, personal contact, item custody, financial follow-up, and telephone burden have been recognized as separate risks.
- The service relationship is framed as an organizational process rather than a personal assignment.
- Transition language avoids promising a specific individual replacement.

**Completion condition**

- Service boundaries, approved exceptions, records, handoff ownership, and escalation routes are organizationally defined.

## K — Patient value and KPI

### K-01: KPI hierarchy

**Purpose**  
Measure patient value and operating performance without allowing one visible count to distort the whole workflow.

**Work completed / established**

- Outcome measures are separated from process measures.
- The KPI chain is viewed as: eligible opportunities → appropriate approach → informed agreement → valid service delivery/claim.
- Productivity and staff-rest constraints are considered when changing approach volume.

**Current issue**

- Aggregation alone did not consistently change behavior.
- A temporary intensive method improved visible output but created sustainability concerns.
- The most useful denominator and the practical opportunity count require continuing validation.

**Next actions**

- Review rates and denominators before raw counts.
- Select one controllable bottleneck each week.
- Give each KPI owner one action and one review date.

**Completion condition**

- Weekly review identifies the bottleneck, action, owner role, and observed result.

### K-02: Short feedback cycle

**Purpose**  
Convert measurement into individual learning with minimal administrative burden.

**Work completed / established**

- Short recurring feedback conversations have been designed.
- Feedback records are limited to the next concrete action rather than long narrative reports.
- Support roles can assist, while accountability remains with the domain owner.

**Completion condition**

- Every frontline member receives periodic feedback and has one current, observable action.

## O — Flow and productivity

### O-01: Separate service wait time from internal processing time

**Purpose**  
Prevent incorrect conclusions caused by treating two different time measures as the same outcome.

**Established definitions**

- **Service wait time:** begins when the customer is received and ends when the customer is called.
- **Internal processing time:** begins when order/prescription data entry starts and ends after the post-service administrative completion step.

These intervals have different start points, end points, and operational meanings.

**Analysis rule**

- Never use internal processing time as a direct substitute for customer wait time.
- Compare interventions against both measures when possible.
- Interpret discrepancies as clues about queueing, pre-entry delay, counseling allocation, post-service work, or measurement coverage.

**Completion condition**

- All reports label the time definition, source, inclusion rule, and missingness.

### O-02: Weekday and time-band analysis

**Purpose**  
Find when demand-capacity mismatch occurs and where intervention is controllable.

**Work completed / established**

- Analyses prioritize ordinary weekdays.
- Saturdays and holidays are excluded from the main model because their operating patterns differ.
- Workload is examined by weekday and time band rather than daily averages alone.

**Next actions**

- Compare demand, active capacity, wait time, and internal processing time by fixed time bands.
- Identify whether the constraint is reception, input, preparation, review, counseling, or downstream completion.
- Test one change at the most constrained interval.

**Completion condition**

- A reproducible weekday report identifies the constrained interval and the outcome of one tested intervention.

### O-03: Off-site work and overlapping time records

**Purpose**  
Avoid overstating available on-site capacity.

**Work completed / established**

- Off-site assignments are recognized as overlapping records attached to the same employee's attendance record.
- Individual community work and facility work are treated as operational activity codes, not separate people.
- Capacity analysis subtracts off-site intervals from on-site availability without double-counting paid hours.

**Completion condition**

- Staffing capacity reconciles attendance, off-site overlap, breaks, and role assignment.

### O-04: Peak-period role allocation

**Purpose**  
Place skilled staff where bottlenecks and decision demand are highest.

**Work completed / established**

- Experienced staff coverage during early/critical periods has been proposed.
- Light and complex service pathways are recognized as requiring different allocation.
- Real-time waiting visibility is considered useful for active redeployment.

**Completion condition**

- Peak intervals have predefined role coverage and a trigger for redeployment.

### O-05: Lightweight operational dashboard

**Purpose**  
Support decisions during the shift without producing another reporting burden.

**Next actions**

- Keep a waiting-status view continuously visible where operationally permitted.
- Limit the dashboard to actionable indicators.
- Review whether the display changed staffing or queue decisions.

**Completion condition**

- The dashboard is used for a documented decision and does not require duplicate manual entry.

## D — Development and delegation

### D-01: Delegation to experienced staff

**Purpose**  
Reduce leader-only work while preserving safety and formal accountability.

**Work completed / established**

- Routine checks and defined operational decisions are being moved to experienced staff.
- High-risk controlled work remains with authorized roles.
- Delegation is paired with rules, escalation triggers, and feedback.

**Completion condition**

- Delegated work has a written boundary, competent assignees, and exception review.

### D-02: OJT operating system

**Purpose**  
Make training progress visible and reduce dependence on informal teaching.

**Work completed / established**

- Training areas include reception, data entry, preparation, review, counseling, inventory, incident prevention, and KPI-related communication.
- Progress conversations identify one concrete next behavior.
- Training structure is linked with daily operations rather than treated as a separate project.

**Completion condition**

- Each learner has a current competency state, next action, and reviewer role.

### D-03: Early-career staff support

**Purpose**  
Address task switching, checking behavior, confidence, and communication before problems accumulate.

**Work completed / established**

- Short interviews have been used to review workload, mistakes, learning barriers, and goals.
- Concrete controls such as a small fixed-point check are preferred to vague instructions to “be careful.”
- Psychological safety and self-efficacy are considered alongside performance expectations.

**Completion condition**

- Agreed behaviors are observable in practice and reviewed without increasing hidden workload.

### D-04: Improvement reporting

**Purpose**  
Build improvement habits across experience levels.

**Work completed / established**

- Experienced staff are expected to submit operational improvements.
- Early-career staff can begin with small environmental or standardization improvements.
- Reporting is intentionally lightweight.

**Completion condition**

- Improvement reports result in adoption, testing, or explicit rejection with a reason.

## R — Research and analytics

### R-01: Training-effect evaluation

**Purpose**  
Evaluate whether education changes practice over time.

**Design direction**

- Repeated measures and clustering are considered.
- Mixed-effects models and generalized mixed models are candidate methods.
- Outcome definition, exposure timing, missingness, and secular trends require explicit handling.

**Completion condition**

- A protocol defines population, intervention, comparator/time structure, outcomes, covariates, missing-data handling, and sensitivity analyses.

### R-02: Documentation-quality evaluation using AI

**Purpose**  
Assess whether AI-assisted documentation improves completeness and clinically meaningful content.

**Design direction**

- Begin with a small feasibility sample before scale-up.
- Separate extraction accuracy, documentation quality, time burden, and patient value.
- Use anonymization, access control, and data-minimization requirements before analysis.

**Completion condition**

- Feasibility, privacy, evaluation rubric, human review, and expansion criteria are documented.

### R-03: Adverse-event signal exploration

**Purpose**  
Explore whether co-reported medicine combinations show signals worth further clinical investigation.

**Design direction**

- Disproportionality results are treated as reporting signals, not incidence, causality, or individual risk.
- Confounding, indication, stimulated reporting, duplicate reports, exposure uncertainty, and reporting volume are explicit limitations.
- Results are translated into verification and follow-up questions rather than prescribing claims.

**Completion condition**

- The analysis plan, reproducible code, cautious interpretation language, and validation path are fixed before reporting.

### R-04: Patient-value evaluation of AI introduction

**Purpose**  
Evaluate AI by what useful patient information or action it enables, not only by time saved.

**Design direction**

- Candidate measures include clinically relevant background capture, problem detection, follow-up action, and documentation burden.
- Baseline measurement and unintended effects are required.

**Completion condition**

- Patient-value and operational outcomes are jointly defined with a privacy-safe data route.

## A — AI and knowledge management

### A-01: Reusable knowledge architecture

**Purpose**  
Prevent useful decisions and learning from being lost across chats, meetings, and documents.

**Work completed / established**

- Daily operations, approved decisions, reusable knowledge, and research materials are treated as different information layers.
- Public-safe GitHub content is used only as a bridge; confidential detail stays in approved private locations.
- Long-form recordings have been converted into structured minutes, decision requests, and action lists.

**Completion condition**

- Each output has one authoritative location and avoids parallel conflicting copies.

### A-02: Daily and weekly decision support

**Purpose**  
Keep briefings actionable and aligned with actual work.

**Work completed / established**

- Daily decision support emphasizes the most important item, three priorities, an on-site check, and work to delegate.
- A weekly evidence watch supports clinical and research planning.
- Uncompleted tasks should remain visible; new suggestions should not bury them.

**Completion condition**

- The system reports actual state first and adds new work only when capacity and priority justify it.

### A-03: Public GitHub to internal AI bridge

**Purpose**  
Allow an internal AI tool to read non-confidential operational logic without transferring internal data to a public service.

**Work completed / established**

- Direct file and raw-text URLs have been successfully read by the internal tool.
- Public content is limited to abstract tasks, definitions, and reusable methods.
- Internal data can be analyzed only inside approved environments; it must not be uploaded here.

**Completion condition**

- The internal tool can reliably retrieve the intended file, follow the safety instruction, and produce a useful output without private details.

## T — Execution system

### T-01: Minimal-update task control

**Purpose**  
Keep the task list aligned with reality without requiring extensive progress entry.

**Work completed / established**

- A previous failure mode was identified: suggestions continued to accumulate while completion state was not updated.
- The new rule is one observable action, one owner role, one review point, and a closure decision.
- Narrative history belongs in this register; active execution belongs in TASKS.md.

**Completion condition**

- Active tasks reflect real work, completed tasks are closed, and stale items are reviewed rather than copied forward.

### T-02: Decision log discipline

**Purpose**  
Avoid repeated debate and conflicting sources of truth.

**Next actions**

- Record only decisions that materially constrain future work.
- Link the decision to the affected task code.
- Replace or supersede old decisions explicitly.

**Completion condition**

- One authoritative decision exists for each active policy question.
